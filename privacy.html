Privacy Policy.

Lino — Daily Journal & Diary · Last updated: September 2026

Lino is a private journaling app built to help you write one honest line a day. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what we handle and why.

Your journal stays on your device

Lino is local-first. Your daily lines, notes, photos, voice recordings, letters, and goals are stored on your device. We do not upload or read your journal content.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to sign in with Apple, so we can give you an account and unlock Plus. Sign in with Apple may provide a private relay email. Used only to run your account.
  • Purchase information — when you subscribe or buy Lino Plus, our payments partner (RevenueCat) processes purchase records tied to an account identifier, to manage your subscription. Payments themselves are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.
  • Account identifier — an ID used to associate your purchases and (optional) account.
  • Approximate location — if you enable it, Lino briefly uses your coarse location to fetch the day's weather. It is used only to get the weather and is not stored as coordinates.

What we do NOT do

  • No ads. No advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data. Ever.
  • We do not read your journal entries.

iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud Backup, your journal is stored in your own iCloud account, managed by Apple under Apple's privacy terms. It is not stored on Lino's servers.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers strictly to run the app: Apple (Sign in with Apple, iCloud, payments), Supabase (account sign-in), RevenueCat (subscription management), and a weather provider (to turn approximate location into a weather reading). Each receives only the minimum needed.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can export your journal any time from Settings → Backup → Export. You can permanently delete everything from Settings → Advanced → Delete my account & data, which erases your entries from the device and deletes your associated account data. There is no undo.

Children

Lino is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Security

Your journal can be locked with Face ID / passcode. Data handled by our providers is protected in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep our footprint small on purpose.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
