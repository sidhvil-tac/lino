Common questions

How do I back up my journal?

Open Settings → Backup. Turn on iCloud Backup to keep a copy in your own iCloud, and use Export my journal to save a copy you can keep or move to a new phone.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and turn on iCloud Backup, or import the file you exported earlier.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Your daily line stays free.

What is Lino Plus?

Plus unlocks voice lines, goals, unlimited letters, every theme, and custom app icons. Your first 60 days of everything are free.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Advanced → Delete my account & data. This permanently erases everything — there is no undo. You can export first if you'd like to keep a copy.
